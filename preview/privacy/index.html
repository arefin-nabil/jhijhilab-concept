Privacy notice

Project enquiries

When you send a project enquiry, jhijhilab receives the name, email address and project details you provide. We use these details to respond and discuss your project.

Email and downloads

If you open an email draft, your email provider handles the draft and delivers it when you send it. If the direct-send option is enabled, the site sends your brief to our inbox through Resend. Downloaded briefs are created in your browser.

Site activity

The interactive lab runs in your browser. It uses sample data. The site does not set analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting provider can process technical request information to operate the site.

Questions about your data

For questions about an enquiry or to request deletion of your enquiry details, contact the studio by email.
